# SPBU brand research

## Source reviewed

The official public website reviewed was [spbu.ru](https://spbu.ru/). The site identifies the institution as Санкт-Петербургский государственный университет / Saint Petersburg State University and uses a formal editorial university presentation with sections for programs, news, events, institutional metrics, contacts, and privacy information.

## Asset discovery

The homepage HTML references an official logo asset at `/themes/spbgu/markup/dist/img/logo-big.svg` and a compact text logo at `/themes/spbgu/markup/dist/img/logo-text-smal.svg`. These are references for institutional research only. They are not copied into the product without explicit permission or an approved asset handoff.

## Visual translation decision

The first-release SPBU//FANTASY interface keeps the product's requested cyberpunk direction while borrowing the official site's institutional qualities: formal hierarchy, restrained editorial structure, high information density, and clear public/private boundaries. The product uses its own text-first mark, `SPBU//FANTASY`, with cyan/pink neon accents on a near-black base. This is a product mark inspired by the university context, not a claim of official institutional endorsement.

## Implementation note

No reliable official color-token list was exposed by the public homepage extraction. Therefore, the product does not claim exact official SPBU brand colors. Any future use of official logo files, exact color values, or formal brand typography should follow an approved asset or brand-guideline handoff from the university.
